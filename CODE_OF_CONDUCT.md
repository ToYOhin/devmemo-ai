# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in DevMemo AI to be respectful, constructive, and safe
for contributors and users of every background and experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Focus feedback on ideas, code, and observable behavior.
- Protect private data, credentials, and security-sensitive details.
- Welcome correction and help maintain an inclusive project space.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information, credentials, or security details without
  permission.
- Deliberately misleading users about DevMemo AI's relationship with Memos.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide, or remove content and limit participation when
needed to protect the community. Report concerns through this repository with
only the minimum non-sensitive detail needed to begin review. Do not publish
private personal information. A dedicated private reporting channel will be
published before any broad public release.
